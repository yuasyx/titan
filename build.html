— The Process

Design and build
under one roof.

TITAN is an end-to-end studio. We take a brief and a plot of land and return a fully printed, serviced home. No subcontractors, no coordination risk, no surprises.

Week 1–2
Brief &
Design
Design team2 architects + 1 engineer
We translate your brief into a print-ready architectural model. Our design team works within the parameters of the TITAN-X system to ensure every feature is printable.
Week 3–4
Print Path
Generation
BuildOSAutomated + reviewed
BuildOS converts the architectural model into a print path in under 2 hours. Our engineers review and sign off every layer sequence before the machine starts.
Week 5–6
Site Prep
& Gantry
On-site team2 technicians
Foundation pad levelled, gantry rails anchored, material feed connected. All done in 6 hours on a flat site. No heavy machinery, no large crew.
Week 6–7
Print
7 Days
TITAN-XContinuous operation
The gantry prints 24 hours a day. Two technicians monitor remotely via BuildOS. Each layer is scanned before the next begins. The structure is complete in 7 days.
Week 8–12
MEP &
Handover
Full fit-out30-day target
MEP rough-in begins day one after print. The building is weathertight from the start. Electrical, plumbing, HVAC installed. Handover with full quality documentation.
