— Our Thesis

The Gulf needs
better homes,
faster.

TITAN was founded in 2024 with a single thesis: the Gulf has a structural housing deficit and no scalable answer. Traditional construction is too slow, too expensive, and too wasteful for the climate.

We built TITAN-X to print the next generation of homes — climate-tuned, fast to deliver, built to last a century. We are headquartered in Dubai Design District and operate across the GCC.
