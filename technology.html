— The System

One machine.
Every layer.

The TITAN-X is a robotic gantry system that deposits mineral concrete in precise horizontal passes. Two technicians operate the entire site. No cranes, no formwork, no waste.

01 / Hardware
Robotic
Gantry
A 3-axis gantry system spanning up to 14m. Precision-controlled nozzle deposits concrete at 150mm/sec with ±2mm accuracy across the full build envelope.
02 / Software
BuildOS
Platform
Our proprietary control platform converts architectural drawings into print paths in under 2 hours. Real-time monitoring, layer verification, and quality logging built in.
03 / Material
Mineral
Concrete M1
A proprietary mineral mix engineered for the Gulf climate. 40MPa compressive strength, UV resistant, zero shrinkage. Formulated for temperatures up to 55°C.
04 / Quality
Layer
Verification
Each layer is scanned at 0.1mm resolution before the next pass begins. Deviations trigger automatic correction. Every build comes with a full quality report.

— The Print Process

From brief
to structure,
in 7 days.

A typical 250m² home follows a precise sequence. Every hour of every day is accounted for. No surprises, no delays.

D+0
Site preparation & gantry assembly
Level the pad, anchor the gantry rails, connect power and material feed. Two technicians, 6 hours.
D+1
Foundation slab print
300mm reinforced slab in a single continuous pass. Cure overnight under shade membrane.
D+2–4
Wall structure — layer by layer
Exterior and load-bearing walls rise at ~18 courses per day. Window voids and service chases printed in-place.
D+5
Roof structure
Monolithic roof shell printed in a single session. Heat-reflective mineral finish applied simultaneously.
D+6–7
Finishing & quality sign-off
Final scan, layer-by-layer quality report issued. Structure handed over — weathertight and ready for MEP.
